# Beta and production store distribution readiness — accepted decision

For [Beta and production store distribution readiness](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/45). The owner accepted the integrated contract through Q12 in the live discussion. Planning only: no account setup, invitations, submission, paid provisioning, or release is authorized here.

## Accepted in the live discussion

- **Q1 — Existing accounts:** Neither Apple Developer nor Google Play Console account exists. The owner identified their personal brand and MMGJLegacy as possible publishers.
- **Q2 — Release ownership:** The owner reviews the evidence and separately authorizes external beta invitations, production access, and paid billing. Agreement to this responsibility is not authorization to execute those stages now.
- **Q3 — Enrollment direction:** The owner agreed with the recommendation to use organization enrollment for MMGJLegacy if it is the publishing legal entity. Its actual legal-entity status and exact registered name have not yet been confirmed; do not represent a brand name as a verified entity.
- **Q4 — Store identity:** Use one store app identity per platform for the disposable beta and production. Production replaces the beta installation; side-by-side beta and production installation is not required. Preserve isolated services, credentials, account identities and local-data namespaces. Before production account/billing initialization or uploads, clear retained beta data and require fresh production account creation/sign-in, as already required by the disposable-beta decision.
- **Q5 — Entity status:** The owner answered “Not quite” to whether MMGJLegacy is already a registered legal entity. Treat organization eligibility as incomplete/unverified; no entity type, registration or enrollment readiness has been established.
- **Q6 — US beta cohort:** Limit invitations to testers the owner knows use US store accounts, without adding residence declarations or location tracking. Verify available channel controls during setup; this does not establish a TestFlight country filter or guarantee store-country eligibility from an invitation alone.
- **Q7 — Coordinated launch:** Public launch waits until both iOS and Android are ready, followed by the owner's coordinated release authorization. Platform review may proceed independently; exact simultaneous store availability has not been promised.
- **Q8 — No formation prerequisite:** The owner rejected waiting for MMGJLegacy organization eligibility with no personal fallback. Plan for organization enrollment if eligible when setup starts, otherwise personal enrollment; verify the actual legal publisher and account details at setup. Do not claim MMGJLegacy organization enrollment is already possible or promise an automatic later account conversion/transfer.
- **Q9 — Billing holds public launch:** Unresolved production-billing qualifications hold the entire public launch, preserving the accepted free/premium offering. Sandbox beta may proceed once its own requirements pass. This includes qualification of remaining paid time and renewals after permanent account erasure; no silent free-only launch or custom purchase-transfer feature is accepted.
- **Q10 — Signing custody:** Use Expo's build service to manage signing credentials, with the owner retaining publisher/build-account ownership and recovery access. Use Google Play App Signing for Android.
- **Q11 — Reviewer access:** Provide dedicated reviewer accounts with synthetic records, representative community strategies and free premium access, plus reliable login and instructions for all three games, subscriptions, reporting/blocking and deletion.

- **Q12 — Integrated review:** The owner accepted the complete release contract, including the verification matrix, stage ownership, failure/retest rules and bounded implementation qualifications.

## Integrated release contract

The following operational details consolidate the accepted choices and platform evidence. Implementation must produce the evidence; this document does not assert that any check passed.

### Accepted content-rights handoff

[Built-in and reference content rights](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/47#issuecomment-5669433821) now supplies the app-content sourcing contract. Before external beta and production distribution, verify the included content inventory, actual permissions/tool terms and offline credits. Use original or suitable free replacements for uncleared items; if required launch coverage cannot be preserved, hold the affected release and return the concrete choice to the owner. Numeric blackjack correctness remains a separate qualification. The source channels' inclusion in credits does not establish a reuse license or endorsement.

### Publisher, identities and build control

Use MMGJLegacy organization enrollment if it is eligible when setup begins; otherwise use the owner's personal enrollment without waiting for formation. Before enrollment, verify the actual legal name, account type, public publisher/contact information, required identity documents and, for organizations, D-U-N-S information and authority. Personal enrollment is not permission to represent an unformed organization as the legal seller. A later conversion/transfer is a separately qualified operation, not a promised automatic step.

The owner controls Apple Developer, Play Console and Expo project access, recovery methods and release permissions. EAS manages Apple distribution credentials and the Android upload key. Google holds the Play app signing key; record both signing and upload certificate fingerprints so OAuth/API configuration uses the correct certificate. Keep secure recovery copies of exportable credentials and document provider recovery for non-exportable keys. Submission service-account credentials are separate from signing credentials; limit their scope. Do not store secrets in this repo or issue comments. Expo usage/tier and any resulting cost are verified at implementation setup; this planning choice is not paid provisioning.

Reserve one stable iOS bundle ID and one Android application ID under the actual publisher's controlled namespace. Record store records, build project, environment identifiers, version/build numbers and source revision together. Keep build/version progression valid across beta and production; Android production must outrank the retired beta version code for the intended update path. Do not promote the sandbox-configured binary unchanged to production. Qualify production environment selection, signing, callbacks and purchase verification in the actual release artifact.

Preserve accepted iPhone/iPad and Android phone/tablet support. Verify supported devices, current upload SDK/target-API requirements, architectures, required capabilities and store device exclusions against the accepted device/accessibility contract. Do not silently enable additional desktop/headset distribution or raise the supported OS floor through store defaults or an SDK upgrade.

### Beta channels and lifecycle

Use email-invited external TestFlight and the initial Google Play closed testing track, with no public invitation links or open-testing campaign. The owner recruits testers known to use US store accounts; use US Play-country targeting on the closed track and verify it independently from production. TestFlight invitation documentation does not establish a country filter. Record the actual controls, without claiming invitations prove eligibility or introducing residence declarations/location tracking. Store reviewers are not restricted by a US physical-location check.

Before invitations, complete enrollment/verification, required app setup, external-beta review, audience/privacy checks and beta billing qualification. If a personal Google account is used, complete the applicable physical-device verification and the 12-testers/14-continuous-days closed test before applying for production access. Maintain real engagement and feedback evidence; satisfying the count alone is not approval. Testers must use their invited account and complete Play opt-in. Android purchasers must separately be license testers and see test payment methods; stop the purchase exercise if the wrong account or real-payment path is shown. TestFlight uses sandbox purchases. Never use a test-track invitation as proof that Android billing is sandboxed.

Maintain invitation membership in both store channels and the beta service. Remove departed testers from the applicable groups/lists and deny further server access; store removal is not local erasure. Preserve the existing offline-access limits rather than promising instant revocation of disconnected installations. Monitor TestFlight's 90-day build expiry and provide a reviewed replacement before expiry while beta continues. Expiry is not a substitute for the accepted shutdown/export notice. For replaced/expired builds, give channel-specific install/update guidance and an available authenticated support/export route until the accepted cutoff.

At shutdown, follow the accepted owner-selected timestamp and at least seven days' email/in-app notice, export opportunity, channel retirement and environment-scoped cleanup. Test delayed/offline devices and restored OS backups. Retire beta authorization, jobs and webhooks; a stale callback, receipt or old app must not recreate beta accounts or touch production. The same-identity update clears beta-local credentials, data, keys and pending work before production account/billing initialization or upload. Users create fresh production accounts; beta entitlements and data never migrate.

### Store submission and reviewer package

Prepare accurate English store descriptions, categories, icons, phone/tablet screenshots, support contact/URL, privacy and deletion URLs, terms and subscription disclosures. Verify applicable agreements, tax/banking setup, export/encryption declarations, SDK/privacy declarations, required permissions and current submission toolchain requirements against the actual binary. Store-facing materials describe blackjack, standard craps, roulette, counting challenges and private casino-session tracking as implemented, with no cash wagering, redeemable prizes or casino-hand tracking. Do not promise financial returns or conceal counting-related functionality.

Answer Apple age/content and Google IARC/target-audience questionnaires truthfully from actual content, including simulated gambling. Consume the accepted 13+ product policy subject to platform restrictions; do not select a lower content rating to force teen availability. Apple's restriction on illegal gambling aids requires qualifying the actual practice-only behavior, not assuming every counting trainer is approved or prohibited. Preserve the accepted gambling-ad block and community moderation/reporting/blocking requirements. Built-in/reference content rights remain separate map scope; this ticket does not establish licensing approval.

Provide persistent, reusable reviewer access valid wherever the platform conducts review, without dependence on a developer answering an email code in real time. Ordinary users retain Apple/Google/email-code sign-in. Any reusable review credential must be server-scoped to designated synthetic test accounts; no universal code, public authentication bypass, access to another user's data or elevated service/admin permission. Explain this access arrangement to reviewers. Keep credentials current in the consoles for every review, store them securely and test them before submission.

Provide both free and premium review paths and spare disposable accounts for deletion tests. Seed representative records and community examples without presenting synthetic activity as organic public rankings/results. Reviewers exercise the same game rules, feature behavior, eligibility protections and moderation flows as users. Include instructions for sign-in, all games/challenges, offline setup, free allowances, subscriptions/restoration, community reporting/blocking, exports and both deletion options. Premium review access is not evidence that store billing works: give separate purchase-test instructions, and submit initial subscription products with the app where required. Keep review backends available through the review period; replace any deleted test account and update its credentials before another submission.

### Release stages and evidence ownership

The implementing developer collects technical evidence. The owner verifies publisher/console facts, reviews the complete evidence and owns each stage authorization. Any delegated reviewer is named in the evidence record. Each result identifies the app version/build, source revision, environment, relevant configuration/policy versions, device/OS where applicable, date, performer and result. Keep secrets and real private records out of reports.

| Stage | Required evidence before owner authorization |
| --- | --- |
| External beta invitations | Verified enrollment and channel setup/review; invitations and US controls; actual sandbox purchase/account checks; beta isolation, recovery and shutdown behavior; applicable audience/privacy/community protections and supported-device checks for the invited build. |
| Production billing qualification | Actual production integration and subscription products; verified ownership/restoration/new-purchase/deletion behavior; supported handling of paid time and renewals after permanent erasure, including cancellation/refund limits. No beta receipt or review entitlement is accepted as production purchase evidence. Any necessary live transaction/provider-contact exercise requires its own implementation authorization. |
| Public durable-service launch | Both platforms ready and approved; billing qualification passed; complete MVP and inherited device/privacy/release checks; beta-to-production transition verified; accurate store materials and working reviewer/support/deletion paths; owner authorizes coordinated public release. |

Hold Apple approval with manual release. On Google, first publish the same app identity to closed testing, then verify Managed Publishing is available/enabled before sending the production release for review. Google does not offer Managed Publishing for the app's first publication. Inspect exceptions such as price/product and membership changes separately; do not assume all console writes are held. If release controls cannot hold production as intended, stop before a publishing action and return the concrete conflict. Independent review is permitted; public launch waits for both platforms, with no promise of simultaneous store propagation.

Neither an approved listing nor successful beta authorizes production accounts or billing. Public launch waits for billing readiness; do not silently launch free-only. Durable service starts with the first non-test account, including free accounts, and routine beta-style resets end. Independent backup/recovery automation retains its accepted post-MVP sequencing; it is not a new prerequisite here. Optional ads or optional analytics remain off when their accepted qualifications have not passed, without blocking the otherwise accepted no-ad/no-analytics path.

### Bounded release-verification matrix

Run these distribution/transition scenarios in addition to the existing device/accessibility and privacy-owner matrices. Use actual signed channel builds for store/install/billing behavior; use controlled test accounts and provider-supported testing where applicable.

| Scenario | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Clean beta install, forwarded invitation URL, removed tester | Only authorized invited accounts join beta; removal denies server access. A shared URL alone does not authorize membership. |
| US/non-US Play accounts and beta/production country settings | Actual eligible-account behavior matches the configured US track/store scope. Record TestFlight's qualified control limitations rather than claiming an untested geofence. |
| Android wrong purchasing account or missing license-tester status | Detect and stop before completing the purchase; no real charge accepted as a beta test. |
| TestFlight expiry, replacement build and Play track retirement | Accurate install/update guidance, required notice/export opportunity, no reliance on uninstall or build expiry for service cleanup. |
| Production update preserves beta storage; fresh install; reinstall/OS restore | Before production initialization/upload, no beta credentials/data/keys/pending work remain active. Fresh production sign-in creates/uses only production identity. |
| Delayed beta device, stale credentials, webhook or job after shutdown | Retired beta work cannot recreate accounts, affect production or grant benefits. Enforce local cleanup when retirement is learned. |
| Sandbox/test receipt or premium review entitlement reaches production verification | No production purchase grant; environment and ownership are verified independently. |
| Existing owner restores, revoked device returns, scheduled deletion is undone | Restore only current verified benefits for the authenticated owner. The retained 30-day account remains the owner; no duplicate ownership or replenished allowance. |
| Permanent erasure followed by historical restore, provider re-ingestion or genuinely new purchase | No private-data resurrection, guessed ownership or silent transfer. New verified purchases remain distinguishable; a conflict blocks billing qualification. |
| Paid time remains at erasure, renewal arrives later, cancellation/refund fails | Supported handling established before billing. Erasure is not cancellation or a guaranteed refund; no unsupported denial of an unresolved paid charge. |
| Reviewer login, premium/free access, report/block, export and deletion | Reliable representative access, functional controls and spare accounts for destructive review exercises; no real-user data or broad auth bypass. |
| Teen/unresolved age, adult privacy choice, premium/offline/failed ad configuration | Existing eligibility/privacy and no-ad rules hold in actual SDK initialization and payloads; no optional collection through an unqualified configuration. |
| Store content rating, screenshots, supported devices, product metadata or app behavior mismatch | Correct the affected configuration/materials or return the explicit product conflict before release. |

Use the accepted full physical-device/accessibility matrix for public launch. Exercise install/update/billing on old and current supported OS representatives for each platform, including a tablet; record any store/native-adapter constraint on the remaining coverage roles. Simulators do not prove store billing, physical performance or supported-device behavior.

### Failure, retest and outstanding qualifications

A required failure blocks its applicable stage. Fix and rerun the affected scenario and dependent checks. Reverify after changes to signing/identity, build/native SDKs, environment endpoints, authentication, billing/ownership, privacy/age/ad behavior, supported-device configuration or store-facing declarations; obtain another store review when required. Do not repeat unrelated checks merely to accumulate evidence. After release, use the existing human incident response and scoped feature controls while correcting/reviewing a replacement; unpublishing does not erase installed apps or repair their state.

The paid-erasure policy remains a focused qualification: establish supported handling of both unused paid time and post-erasure renewal, applicable refund/cancellation remedies, and actual RevenueCat/store ownership behavior. Public documentation checked does not directly settle intentional no-refund forfeiture. If the intended contract cannot be supported, return the precise product choice for acceptance; do not silently add custom transfer, permanent hidden retention or automatic forfeiture. This unresolved implementation/store qualification blocks billing and public launch under Q9; it does not block planning resolution or a qualified sandbox beta.

Exact publisher identity/eligibility, enrollment documents, identifiers, credentials, reviewer access mechanics, console controls, current ratings/toolchain/SDK declarations and physical/store tests must be qualified during implementation. These are checks against the contract, not claims already established or new planning tickets. Built-in/reference content rights, onboarding/tutorial curriculum and implementation sequencing remain outside this decision.

## Evidence consulted

- [Apple enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/): organizations require legal-entity status, binding authority, D-U-N-S identification and organization contact/web information; individual enrollment displays the individual's legal name as seller.
- [Google account types](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885?hl=en): distinguishes personal and organization enrollment, including organization D-U-N-S requirements.
- [Apple external testers](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/invite-external-testers): invitation/review controls; no country filter established by the documentation consulted.
- [Google country targeting](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/7550024?hl=en): targeting is based on Play account country, not current physical location.
- [Google new personal-account testing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en): at least 12 closed testers continuously opted in for the preceding 14 days before applying for production access; approval is separate and additional testing may be required. Apply this prerequisite if personal enrollment is used, and retain substantive engagement/feedback evidence.
- [Google device verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14316361?hl=en), [Play test tracks](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en), [Play billing testing](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/test), [TestFlight tester guidance](https://testflight.apple.com/): account/channel setup, opt-in, installation persistence, sandbox limits and build expiry.
- [Expo app credentials](https://docs.expo.dev/app-signing/app-credentials/), [Expo signing security](https://docs.expo.dev/app-signing/security/), [Android signing](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing): signing ownership, hosted credential custody and upload versus distribution keys.
- [Apple manual release](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-apps-availability/select-an-app-store-version-release-option/), [Google publishing controls](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859654?hl=en): owner-controlled release, first-publication limitation and exceptions.
- [Google reviewer access](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15748846?hl=en), [Apple review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Apple initial IAP submission](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-in-app-purchase): reusable access, complete reviewable functionality and billing submission requirements.
- [Apple content-rating definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/), [Google content ratings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655), [Google gambling policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032): truthful classification and restrictions based on actual behavior.
- [Apple deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/), [Apple agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/), [Google subscriptions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533), [Google purchase management](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/manage-purchases), [RevenueCat customer deletion](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/customer-profile), [RevenueCat restore behavior](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/projects/restore-behavior): deletion does not itself cancel subscriptions or prove permanent forfeiture; provider re-ingestion and ownership need actual qualification. See also the focused [erasure investigation](../research/subscription-erasure-forfeiture.md).

The accepted audience, privacy, device/accessibility, disposable-beta and subscription decisions remain inputs. No successful store review, console configuration, SDK integration or physical-device test has been claimed.
