# Built-in and reference content rights — accepted decision

For [Built-in and reference content rights](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/47). The owner accepted the complete contract through Q6 in the live discussion. Planning only: no paid licenses, rights-holder contact or asset publication is authorized.

## Accepted in the live discussion

- **Q1 — Original teaching content:** Use independently implemented strategies, validated calculations, original explanations and original chart designs. The owner selected [Casino Quest](https://www.youtube.com/@CasinoQuest) as a primary research source for roulette and craps, and [Blackjack Apprenticeship](https://www.youtube.com/blackjackapprenticeship) as a primary research source for blackjack. These preferences do not establish permission to redistribute the channels' videos, transcripts, charts, branding or other expression.
- **Q2 — Third-party assets:** Original assets are the default. There is no current identified need for third-party assets, but they may be considered if a concrete need arises or a sponsor requests their use. This does not select any stock library, grant permission for sponsor materials, or establish a sponsorship arrangement.
- **Q3 — Paid licensing:** Evaluate exceptions case by case, with a strong preference to avoid all paid licenses until product-market fit and profitability. No paid asset/license budget or purchase is approved. Default to original content or a suitable no-fee permission; return a specific cost/rights tradeoff before any exception.
- **Q4 — AI-generated assets:** Original visuals/audio may include AI-generated work, with quality review and recorded usage rights for the chosen tool. No specific generator, voice, asset pack or paid service has been selected.
- **Q5 — Sources & credits:** Include a Sources & credits page linking the channels and specific materials actually used, together with required license notices. Keep research attribution distinct from sponsorship/endorsement. Use external links initially; embedding videos is not part of the accepted plan.
- **Q6 — Integrated review:** Accepted the complete contract, including the minimal provenance inventory, owner review, AI/sponsor qualifications, offline credits, original/free replacement rule, release hold for unresolved required coverage and separate correctness validation.

## Integrated content-rights contract

The following accepted details define a small content inventory and release checks, not a new content-management product or a full legal clearance exercise.

### Source use and original work

Prioritize Casino Quest for roulette/craps teaching research and Blackjack Apprenticeship for blackjack. Other primary references and independent calculations may corroborate facts or fill gaps. Record the exact video/page and relevant timestamp/section for a sourced claim; do not attribute a specific instruction merely to a channel as a whole. Preserve creator-specific variants and identify True Count's own selected caps, resets and other modifications. Do not imply that Casino Quest or BJA authored or approved True Count's launch catalog.

Write original explanations, examples and chart presentation, and independently implement supported game/strategy methods. Reference-only status does not approve downloading and bundling a video, transcript, chart, selected instructional package or source code. Any proposed copying/adaptation requires item-specific rights assessment and permission where needed. Access restrictions and the applicable source/platform terms must be respected; buying a chart, book or membership is not treated as a redistribution license.

Keep the accepted rule presets, count conventions and finite coaching scope. If a source's teaching differs, record the difference and validate the applicable True Count behavior through the existing coaching investigation. Do not silently switch to a simplified source chart, relabel unverified thresholds as certified, or claim that independently recomputing numbers clears every issue involving a selected teaching package or its branding. Any concrete rights conflict with required coverage returns to the owner with options.

### Minimal provenance record

Keep a repository inventory for shipped app-supplied content. A record can cover a coherent asset set under the same provenance/license, with individual files/revisions identified. Do not require a separate administrative record for each chart cell or ordinary sentence.

| Field | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Item and revision | Content/asset identity, included files or generated-data revision, and where used in the app/store materials. |
| Origin | Human author or generator/tool and generation date; research sources with specific URLs, sections/timestamps and date checked; supplied originals if commissioned/sponsored. |
| Rights basis | Original-work basis, applicable tool terms, exact license/version or written grant and its permitted uses. Retain suitable evidence without copying an entire protected source corpus. |
| Usage limits | Commercial app distribution, offline bundling, modifications and store marketing as applicable; attribution, territory/duration and termination obligations if present. |
| Changes and validation | True Count modifications and a separate pointer to mathematical/content validation where needed. Rights approval is not a correctness result. |
| Release review | Owner/reviewer, date, decision and any outstanding qualification. |

The author/implementing developer prepares the record; the owner reviews it before external distribution. No paid asset/license is automatically permitted after profitability either: specific terms and costs still require the case-by-case decision. This content policy does not remove license obligations for software dependencies, system-provided resources or bundled fonts.

### AI-generated and exceptional third-party material

For AI-generated assets, record the tool/service, relevant terms/version or evidence date, generated output revision and material source inputs. Verify that supplied inputs are usable for the proposed purpose and review the output for quality and identifiable third-party material. Permission to use output is distinct from a claim of exclusive ownership or copyright protection. No particular tool's terms or generated asset is approved merely by accepting this policy.

Use third-party assets only for an identified need or accepted sponsor request. A sponsor's request alone does not prove it controls every supplied component. Obtain the grant covering the actual asset, branding, app/store/offline uses, modifications and duration; clarify attribution and what happens when the arrangement ends. Keep research credits separate from sponsor recognition. Specific sponsorship placements, compensation and promotional claims remain a later proposal; do not introduce a sponsorship feature here.

### Sources & credits

Make Sources & credits reachable from Settings/About. Include the selected primary channels and specific references used in shipped teaching, label True Count variants clearly, and distinguish research references from licensed assets and any separately accepted sponsor acknowledgment. Use names/text links without borrowing logos or thumbnails by implication. Show mandatory notices wherever the actual license requires, including additional placement if a central page alone is insufficient.

Bundle the text credits and required notices for offline reading; opening an external source requires connectivity and a deliberate user action. Do not preload third-party players, thumbnails or trackers. No source video is required to complete a built-in exercise. Retain enough title/author/version information for a useful citation if a link later disappears; a broken research link alone does not invalidate independently supported content or justify reproducing the missing material.

### Release checks and unresolved rights

Apply these checks before external beta distribution and again for the actual production content set. Recheck changed content, source inputs, licenses, tool terms relevant to the use or sponsor conditions when they affect a later release. Feed the results into [Beta and production store distribution readiness](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/45), without creating a parallel release process.

| Check | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| App-supplied content and store assets | Every included item/set has a usable rights basis and required notices. Primary-source links alone are not a license. |
| Original explanations/calculated references | Original expression/provenance is recorded; mathematical correctness remains separately verified under the accepted rules. |
| AI-generated or sponsor-supplied asset | Relevant tool/input or sponsor rights cover the actual use, and output/asset review is complete. |
| Offline package and Sources & credits | Required attribution/notices remain available offline; external links are optional and no videos are silently bundled. |
| Missing permission or incompatible conditions | Replace the item with suitable original or no-fee material. Withhold the affected item from external distribution while unresolved. |
| Replacement cannot preserve required launch coverage | Hold the affected release and return the exact licensing, replacement or scope choice to the owner. Do not silently drop a launch game, strategy or required coaching coverage. |

If a credible rights issue is discovered after distribution, the owner investigates the specific item and uses the existing support/release process to correct or replace it as appropriate. Preserve historical result/revision integrity; changing an asset or rights record does not silently rewrite scored sessions. This decision creates no promise to instantly erase content from disconnected devices and does not extend or override the separate community-content removal contract.
