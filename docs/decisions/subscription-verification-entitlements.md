# Subscription verification and entitlement implementation — accepted decision

Decision ticket: [Subscription verification and entitlement implementation](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/23), part of the [MVP decision map](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/1).

The [resolution comment](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/23#issuecomment-5565993635) is canonical; this file preserves the reviewed decision for repository readers.

Accepted through the live Q1–Q8 review, including the integrated design, supplemental current-store verification, operational targets and handoffs. This is planning, not production implementation. Preserve the accepted offline-access, authentication/account-lifecycle, and local-persistence decisions.

## Accepted choices

- **Q1 — Duplicate subscriptions:** prevent starting a second subscription in the app when the account already has premium through either store. Direct the player to manage the existing subscription. If overlapping subscriptions nevertheless occur, provide one premium entitlement, explain the duplicate billing, and offer both store-management links. Do not add subscription durations together.
- **Q2 — Billing channels:** use Apple and Google in-app subscriptions only at launch. Retain the accepted monthly and annual plans and account-level premium across platforms. Web subscription checkout is outside MVP; the accepted minimal web account-deletion path remains in scope.
- **Q3 — Provider:** use RevenueCat for store integration, with True Count's backend controlling purchase ownership and premium access. Disable automatic purchase transfers and qualify the amended no-custom-restoration policy after permanent erasure; provider defaults alone do not satisfy account ownership. If qualification fails, reopen the integration design rather than weaken the accepted lifecycle.
- **Q4 — Billing grace:** configure 16 days on Apple and 7 days on Google. Store billing grace remains distinct from True Count's accepted offline verification deadline.
- **Q5 — Subscription pause:** disable Google's optional subscription pause feature at launch. Cancellation of renewal preserves access through the recognized entitlement end; a player may subscribe again later.
- **Q6 — Billing-cadence changes:** monthly-to-annual and annual-to-monthly changes begin at the next renewal, with uninterrupted premium and no immediate replacement charge.
- **Q7 — Family Sharing:** launch premium covers the purchasing True Count account only. Apple Family Sharing remains disabled. The account's benefits still work across its iOS and Android devices.

- **Q8 — Integrated design:** accepted the complete design below, including direct Apple/Google checks for verification freshness, pending-purchase recovery, single-owner restoration, reconciliation cadences, operational targets, release qualification and the existing retention/recovery handoffs.

## Integrated design — accepted in Q8

The following makes Q1–Q7 and the previously accepted contracts concrete and was accepted in Q8. All verification scenarios below are requirements for future implementation, not tests claimed to have run.

### Store and provider configuration

Use `react-native-purchases` in Expo development/production builds and one RevenueCat project containing the iOS and Android apps. Map all launch products to one premium entitlement. Keep production and sandbox credentials, evidence and access isolated; sandbox purchases cannot grant production premium. Native module/SDK versions must meet the stores' submission requirements at release and pass the matrix selected by [Mobile device support and accessibility](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/25); that decision remains open.

On Apple, place monthly and annual auto-renewable products in one subscription group at the same service level. Enable 16-day billing grace for paid-to-paid renewals, test in sandbox, then enable production. Keep Family Sharing disabled. On Google, use separate monthly and annual subscription products, each with its corresponding auto-renewing base plan, both mapped to the same premium entitlement. This permits explicit `DEFERRED` product replacement in both directions without relying on same-subscription base-plan replacement modes. Include the current product/token in the replacement operation, process/acknowledge the new token when issued, and retain the current entitlement until its scheduled replacement. Configure required Google server notifications for RevenueCat's deferred handling.

Google grace is 7 days on both plans; retain automatic account-hold calculation, currently 60 days minus grace (53 days with this setting). Account hold does not grant premium. Pause stays disabled. Do not offer prepaid, installment, add-on, promotional or win-back products at launch; the accepted free training allowance supplies the preview. Re-enabling renewal of an existing subscription is management of that subscription, not a duplicate purchase. Retain and reconcile supported store-initiated renewals/resubscriptions rather than assuming every transaction started in the app.

Display the store-returned localized total recurring price and billing period, renewal terms, cancellation guidance, links to terms/privacy, Restore purchases and Manage subscription. The accepted provisional US prices remain $5.99/month and $39.99/year with identical benefits and no introductory premium trial. Localized prices and sale regions remain with launch-market planning. The annual total is prominent; a monthly equivalent cannot replace it. State the accepted periodic-verification condition for offline premium. Use original-store management links even on the other platform; overlapping subscriptions need both links rather than RevenueCat's single preferred management URL. Refund requests use the original store's supported process, without promising approval or silently canceling/refunding a purchase.

### Ownership and purchase flow

Use the signed-in account's stable, opaque identifier for RevenueCat; never email or display name. Configure the SDK only for the intended account before purchase/restore, prevent anonymous purchasing, and discard callbacks from an older account-session generation. RevenueCat customer identifiers and client-reported CustomerInfo are not authentication or independent authorization to premium.

The Supabase-backed subscription service owns the mapping from verified store purchase lineage to exactly one True Count account. Distinguish the store subscription and its replacement/renewal transactions from the premium access it contributes. Validate application/product, production versus sandbox, purchase authenticity, and existing ownership before accepting an update. Apple original transaction lineage and Google's linked purchase tokens must survive plan changes without producing a second owner. Store/provider calls happen outside database transactions; the final authorized transaction checks current account/deletion state, ownership generation and operation identity before updating entitlement state and the account synchronization feed.

Preflight purchases online against the account's existing subscriptions. Existing premium directs the player to manage its billing source; an intended same-store plan replacement is distinct from buying a second subscription. Concurrent store operations or purchases outside the app can still create overlaps, so do not promise global prevention. Retain each verified subscription independently and grant one premium benefit while any owned source qualifies; ending one source cannot revoke another valid source or add their durations together.

Bind purchase/restore retries to the original operation and store transaction. A canceled purchase does not change access. A pending store payment does not grant premium. A completed store charge awaiting backend acceptance shows verification pending with retry/restore and support options; do not initiate another charge to resolve it. A failed verification preserves only existing permitted access. Finish/acknowledge store transactions through the supported integration and durably track unresolved delivery; meet Google's acknowledgment deadline and verify this behavior when the application/backend fails. A verification receipt is not a second allowance grant.

### Restoration and deletion

The later [subscription ownership decision](subscription-lineage-retention.md) controls post-erasure behavior and retention, superseding the former custom benefits-only transfer exception. Intended forfeiture requires store/terms qualification before production billing.

The [30-day deletion undo amendment](authentication-account-lifecycle.md#accepted-amendment--30-day-deletion-undo-window) preserves the same account and purchase owner while deletion is scheduled. Canceling scheduled deletion is account reactivation, not a purchase transfer. Permanent erasure no longer offers a custom benefits-only reassignment; the undo window neither pauses billing nor expires a retained account's store entitlement.

Restoring on another device for the same account verifies the original store purchase and reuses its existing ownership. Cross-platform benefits then follow the True Count account; access on Android does not itself prove ownership of an Apple purchase. Explicit store restoration requires a supported device/store session capable of presenting that purchase. Explain the needed original-store access if the current platform cannot supply it.

If the purchase belongs to an existing account, require sign-in to that owner without revealing its identity. Account recovery continues to require a linked sign-in method. Support cannot turn a receipt into access to private records or a discretionary purchase transfer.

After permanent account erasure, do not offer custom reassignment of its existing benefits to a new account. A receipt, webhook or re-created RevenueCat customer cannot recreate the app account or bypass backend ownership. The user intends forfeiture of remaining paid time; this remains a focused store/terms qualification, not a claim that the store entitlement was revoked. Preserve same-account restoration before erasure and separate authenticated new purchases after erasure.

RevenueCat's keep-original-owner setting remains the baseline for retained owners. It is not a documented permanent block after customer deletion. Qualify restore, notification re-ingestion, anonymous aliases and fresh purchases with backend ownership checks. Do not invoke broad privileged transfers or re-ingest an erased customer to recover private identity. If historical attribution is missing or ambiguous, decline automatic reassignment; never resolve an uncertain charge by prompting another payment.

Same-account restoration requires original-store evidence, authenticated True Count intent and current server-side verification. A customer ID, screenshot or support claim is insufficient. Bind evidence to the account/operation and protect tokens from logs and public access. Qualify the current ownership, deletion/re-ingestion and new-purchase cases in the [subscription ownership decision](subscription-lineage-retention.md); unsupported attribution is an integration gap, not permission to assign benefits to an arbitrary account.

### Entitlement normalization and delivery

Keep recognized store access separate from renewal intent, paid-period end, store grace end, offline deadline and verification failure. Active and store-grace states grant access. Cancellation of renewal alone preserves it. Confirmed expiration, revocation or account hold removes that source's access; a refund event must be interpreted according to its actual entitlement effect rather than assumed to revoke everything. Recovery restores only the verified source and owner. Unexpected/contradictory states require reconciliation and cannot manufacture a new offline window.

For confirmed current entitlement with usable end E, preserve the accepted deadline min(V + seven days, E + 72 hours), where V is a successful authoritative verification time. Use the recognized grace end when applicable, not stale paid expiry. A confirmed entitled response without usable E preserves current verified access and any valid previous offline deadline, but issues no new offline extension. Successful negative verification overrides previous grace for that source; failed verification leaves its original deadline unchanged. Aggregate overlapping sources without mixing one source's verification time with another's end.

**Accepted freshness strategy:** retain RevenueCat for purchase handling and lifecycle processing, and use supplemental server-to-store checks to establish V. Apple Get All Subscription Statuses and Google `purchases.subscriptionsv2.get` provide current subscription state. This needs server credentials plus verified Apple transaction lineage / Google purchase tokens; an authenticated server operation must retain these securely when purchasing/restoring, with a qualified native adapter if the selected RevenueCat SDK does not expose the needed evidence. A platform without local store proof can refresh an already-owned subscription through the evidence held by our backend.

RevenueCat's `request_date` is the request date, not a documented last-store-check timestamp. A successful RevenueCat GET, authentic SDK response, temporary provider entitlement, SDK offline grant, duplicate webhook or read of our saved snapshot cannot advance V. Store a separate observation time for such responses. Successful direct verification records the check's conservative server time (request start, accounting for delayed responses), evidence identity, source state, ownership generation and recognized E atomically. Serializing checks per lineage and rejecting stale generations prevents an older response from undoing a newer accepted check or reassignment. A failed direct check preserves prior V and its deadline; it is not successful negative verification. A recognized negative direct response supersedes that source's cached access immediately. A verified store event establishing revocation is also applied without waiting for a failed follow-up refresh; generic provider event labels alone do not establish this effect.

Use Apple's signed renewal grace expiry for an entitled grace state and Google's current entitled line item's expiry, including its dynamically extended grace. Check RevenueCat's explicit V1 `grace_period_expires_date` and V2 `gives_access`/active-entitlement `expires_at` against store fixtures; do not assume billing-period fields include grace or add the configured grace duration to a paid expiry. A pending Google replacement line item with no expiry must not obscure the currently active line item's end. Store-current evidence takes precedence over older provider observations; unexplained disagreements trigger reconciliation and an alert, not an arbitrary extra grace period.

Reading our saved snapshot or receiving a fresh server-time anchor must never advance V. Native elapsed time and boot-continuity checks follow the accepted persistence design. Clock uncertainty requires revalidation of time-limited rights while permitted settlement/history remain available. For the exceptional confirmed-entitled/no-usable-E case, current verified access authorizes the current connected foreground activity; pause/background, connectivity loss or failed required revalidation returns to any still-valid prior offline deadline. Recheck that exceptional state on the connected 60-second cadence. Do not invent a permanent right or reject the currently confirmed entitled response using a stale paid expiry.

Commit entitlement changes through the account-state synchronization boundary. Notify connected devices with a change hint and fetch authoritative state on foreground/reconnection and the accepted 60-second connected cadence. Do not call stores per game turn. The engine receives allowed execution boundaries from the application access layer; it does not import the billing SDK.

Preserve the accepted access-loss rules: finish the current turn, settle outstanding commitments, save, and stop new interactive turns; already-started challenges may finish under their existing exception. A verified upgrade stops further allowance consumption. A free training-bankroll session finishes its current turn and final settlement before new premium practice starts with ordinary funding. Old-grant settlement never refills a new grant. Downgrade uses only a still-valid available free allowance or an authorized new daily grant.

### Notifications, reconciliation and outages

Connect App Store Server Notifications V2 and Google RTDN to the supported RevenueCat integration. Authenticate RevenueCat webhooks with a configured server secret, validate environment/app/product, durably enqueue before acknowledgement, and deduplicate event IDs. Event names such as cancellation, billing issue and product change are triggers to reconcile current state; they are not interchangeable with expiration. Provider transfers never override our ownership table. Missing event delivery is handled by scheduled reconciliation; provider retries alone are insufficient.

Accepted launch cadence: perform a current-store check on purchase/restore acceptance, explicit Recheck access, a relevant lifecycle event, and crossing a known entitlement boundary. On app foreground/reconnection, refresh when the last check is at least six hours old; coalesce concurrent requests per purchase lineage. Ordinary 60-second account synchronization distributes existing authoritative state without polling stores per device every minute. Reconcile all known active/grace/retrying/held subscriptions at least daily, scheduling known renewal/grace boundaries separately and retaining a bounded retry queue for failures. Terminal subscriptions leave routine polling after pending events/recovery work is reconciled; later restore or store events can revalidate them.

The six-hour/daily cadences are operational defaults, not extra offline grace or guarantees of instant discovery. Every successful check may supply a new fixed offline deadline, delivered with its original V; downloading it later cannot move that time. After a current-store check fails, show stale/pending verification while preserving the prior deadline. Retry transient errors with jittered exponential backoff capped at five minutes, respect rate limits, and retain durable retry state. Bound/coalesce explicit rechecks to prevent retry storms without interpreting throttling as non-entitlement.

For online-only premium actions such as publication/rating, the account service authorizes from its accepted recognized entitlement and revalidates stale/expired evidence before allowing a new action. Client offline grace is not authority to write public contributions. A provider/store outage does not erase local records, revoke a still-valid cached offline grant, block permitted settlement, or grant extra allowance. A missing end, malformed response or ambiguous identity remains distinguishable from confirmed expiration. A completed charge awaiting verification stays pending; the app offers retry/restore and original-store support without another purchase prompt.

Accepted internal targets: begin processing accepted webhook work within one minute and alert if unprocessed work exceeds five minutes; propagate an accepted entitlement change to a healthy connected foreground client by its next 60-second account reconciliation. These are implementation targets, not store-delivery guarantees. Track verification failures/age, unacknowledged Google purchases, pending paid activation/restore, provider ownership mismatches, queue lag and reconciliation coverage through the accepted PostHog operational setup. Alert on Google acknowledgement still pending after one hour so recovery can occur before its three-day deadline. Keep access controls, secrets and sensitive tokens out of analytics.

### Retention and recovery handoffs

Retain minimum protected purchase lineage, current owner/generation and entitlement/verification state while the account exists, including its 30-day undo period. At permanent erasure remove the association through normal cleanup and initiate processor deletion, subject only to specifically disclosed necessary exceptions. Retain raw tokens only while needed for verification or unresolved processing. Do not retain private records or a permanent forfeiture blacklist. The [subscription ownership decision](subscription-lineage-retention.md) owns the updated endpoints and qualification boundaries.

[Launch markets and data residency](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/27) must cover RevenueCat/store processing locations, disclosures, applicable billing/privacy obligations and any legally required transaction retention. [Backend operating envelope and disaster recovery](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/28) must settle finite billing/control/evidence retention, provider deletion processing, credential rotation, direct-store verification volume and cost, alert ownership, and restore drills. Include RevenueCat's currently published 1% tracked-revenue charge after its $2,500 monthly threshold, plus the direct-check workload; no provider-specific SLA is assumed.

After a backend restore, keep new premium acceptance and ownership changes behind recovery reconciliation. Restore protected deletion/ownership control state before accepting stale devices or store events; reconstruct current billing from stores and reconcile RevenueCat before issuing new entitlement snapshots. Never reconstruct V from the time a backup was loaded. Existing device offline deadlines remain unchanged while recovery is pending. The operations ticket must test loss of recent restore/deletion receipts and recover the authoritative single-owner result without reviving accounts or duplicating allowance.

### Required scenario acceptance

| Scenario | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase succeeds; backend response is lost or app closes | Same transaction resumes verification, one owner/access update, no second charge; acknowledgement/delivery recovery is monitored. |
| Pending payment, canceled checkout, temporary RevenueCat grant | No invented store-confirmed premium or V; current permitted rights remain. |
| Repeated cached RevenueCat GETs over more than seven days | V does not slide; only a successful qualified current-store check can refresh it. |
| Paid expiry precedes a currently entitled store-grace end | Premium remains; use the recognized grace end in the offline formula. |
| Confirmed entitled with missing/unusable E | Current verified access remains; no manufactured offline extension or stale-paid-end rejection. |
| Failed verification versus confirmed expiration/hold/revocation | Failure preserves prior deadline; a confirmed loss removes that source even before the deadline. |
| Cancellation or refund without loss of entitlement | Apply actual current access state, not a generic cancellation/refund label. |
| Recovery after grace or account hold | Restore the verified entitlement once, without allowance refill or private-account transfer. |
| Monthly/annual switch, new token now and future line item without expiry | No charge before agreed renewal; retain current access, acknowledge correctly, and preserve one lineage/owner. |
| Two subscriptions overlap; one later expires/refunds | One premium benefit remains while another source qualifies; show both management links, never sum durations. |
| Restore same account on a different platform versus new account with existing owner | Previously verified ownership follows the account; existing-owner conflict cannot transfer purchases or expose identity. |
| Permanent erasure, two new accounts submit the old purchase | No automatic reassignment or account resurrection; qualify historical-purchase rejection without duplicate benefits. |
| Provider customer deleted, old receipt restored, then genuinely new purchase | Re-ingestion cannot bypass ownership; qualify a new purchase separately without transferring unrelated purchases or creating an unusable charge. |
| Old webhook or older in-flight verification after reassignment/deletion | No resurrection, rollback of ownership generation or stale entitlement overwrite. |
| Clock change, reboot uncertainty, delayed response or backup reinstall | No deadline extension from displayed time, receipt arrival or restored anchors; required revalidation preserves settlement/history. |
| Upgrade/downgrade mid training-bankroll turn or craps settlement | Finish/settle original funding exactly once; no premium winnings in free allowance and no new-day refill. |
| Missed webhooks, provider/store outage, exhausted retries or rate limits | Durable reconciliation/retry, unchanged cached deadlines, actionable pending state and alerts; no inferred revocation from transport failure. |
| Backup predates deletion or benefit restoration | Reconcile protected ownership/deletion facts before service resumes; do not revive accounts or refresh V from backup time. |
| Sandbox evidence, forged customer ID, replayed token, wrong app/product | No unauthorized production entitlement or cross-account data access. |

Provider deletion/restore, token extraction, replacement semantics, grace fields and direct-store checks require sandbox/device qualification before production billing. A failure reopens the implementation design; it does not silently remove an accepted feature. No production purchase, credential setup or store configuration is performed during this planning ticket.

### Evidence checked during this decision

- [RevenueCat pricing](https://www.revenuecat.com/pricing): current published free threshold and tracked-revenue charge; include provider charges in the backend operating envelope.
- [RevenueCat restore behavior](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/projects/restore-behavior): keep-original-owner rules and receipt-wide effects need explicit account handling.
- [RevenueCat subscription management](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/subscription-guidance/managing-subscriptions): replacement behavior, notification needs and store-management links.
- [Apple billing grace configuration](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/enable-billing-grace-period-for-auto-renewable-subscriptions): 16-day monthly/annual grace is configurable and must be tested before production enablement.
- [Google subscription configuration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/140504?hl=en): grace, account hold and optional pause settings.
- [Google subscription changes](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/subscriptions): same-subscription base-plan replacement constraints differ from deferred replacement across subscription products. Map the chosen player-facing billing behavior to supported SDK/store configuration, not an assumed interchangeable replacement enum.
- [RevenueCat customer model](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v1/customer-info-model): request timestamps and a preferred management URL are not store-check timestamps or a complete list of overlapping subscriptions.
- [RevenueCat grace handling](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/subscription-guidance/how-grace-periods-work): explicit grace fields must be interpreted separately from billing issue/cancellation events.
- [RevenueCat webhook fields](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/integrations/webhooks/event-types-and-fields): temporary entitlement grants are distinct from completed store validation.
- [Apple current subscription status workflow](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10040/?time=657) and [Google subscription lifecycle](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/lifecycle/subscriptions): current-store endpoints support supplemental verification; the combined architecture is our accepted design, not a provider guarantee.
- [Google purchase security](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security): backend verification, purchase token handling and timely acknowledgement.

These documents establish API/configuration facts, not evidence that True Count's implementation has passed the required scenarios.
