# Launch provider privacy disclosures

Research date: 2026-09-11. Evidence for issue #36. This is a planning data-flow register, not completed store declarations or verified production settings. It incorporates the accepted launch advertising and subscription-lineage decisions; older research proposals for permanent purchase-ownership markers are superseded.

The active-system cleanup target begins at the irreversible boundary. Ordinary deletion therefore has a 30-day reversible phase before that boundary; “Delete all my data now” skips it. Provider exceptions and rolling recovery copies must appear separately in user-facing retention language. Ordinary active accounts have no inactivity purge. Do not substitute the older research's proposed 180-day terminal billing evidence or indefinite ownership marker for the newer accepted ownership cleanup.

## Apple and Google declarations

Apple App Privacy answers include third-party SDK practices, purposes, linking and tracking, rather than just fields True Count explicitly sends. Likely data classes to evaluate from the above register include contact details, user identifiers, purchases, user content, product interactions, diagnostics and any coarse location derived from IP; final selections depend on the built app. Apple distinguishes collection from transient servicing of requests, so evaluate real retention instead of guessing from the existence of a network call. [App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).

Privacy manifests/SDK signatures and required-reason API declarations are artifact requirements. Xcode can aggregate SDK manifests, but this does not establish the app's complete privacy label or demonstrate that a configured SDK performs no tracking. Apple ATT covers cross-company data linkage for targeting **or ad measurement**, including SDK behavior the developer does not directly use. The accepted launch excludes an ATT opt-in flow; qualify actual nontracking operation or return the conflict. [SDK requirements](https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/), [ATT scope](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/).

Play Data safety also covers SDK collection/sharing. Determine the applicable service-provider sharing exceptions and ephemeral-processing treatment from actual processing; SDK installation, contextual labeling or encryption alone cannot settle the answers. Required/optional collection and purposes must match the app's working settings and consent/eligibility gates. [Play Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469).

## Minimum remaining product decisions

**Optional analytics:** decide whether MVP offers a separate, default-off product-analytics opt-in or leaves optional analytics disabled. Proposed minimum if enabled: named events only, no replay at launch, no private content, no advertising linkage, a selected finite event-retention period and a working revocation/deletion route. Necessary operational processing should be described separately; toggling optional analytics must not silently disable essential account/security processing. Teens require separate limited-analytics qualification before collection, consistent with accepted eligibility policy.

**Ad privacy behavior:** the accepted fallback is already no ad when eligibility, teen protection or required privacy state cannot be established, and no ad on unavailable inventory. The remaining user-facing choice is how an applicable ad-data opt-out affects contextual delivery: qualified limited/nontracking delivery where supportable, otherwise no request and uninterrupted free training. Do not silently require premium, add personalization/ATT consent, or override a privacy refusal. Google's TEEN treatment disables personalization but only CHILD expressly promises AAID/IDFA suppression in the cited table; verify remaining identifiers independently. [Age treatment](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6219315).

The unresolved PostHog event-retention capability is a concrete provider/implementation conflict, not a reason to invent a longer policy silently. Keep unqualified optional collection off while choosing a supportable schedule. None of this research provisions services, contacts providers, generates live traffic, fills store forms, or proves physical purge.
