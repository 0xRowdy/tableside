# Competitor age eligibility

Research date: 2026-09-08. Related decisions: [Launch age eligibility and geographic access #34](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/34) and [prelaunch age review #42](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/42). This is a bounded comparison of five training and simulation products, building on [launch pricing research](launch-pricing.md).

## Findings

Training without real-money wagering does not, by itself, establish that competitors admit minors. Crapsee explicitly describes itself as an educational simulation with fictional currency while requiring adult eligibility. Conversely, a lower store rating exists in this category: Blue Wind's blackjack trainer is rated 13+ on the US App Store. These observations establish variation in published policies and classifications; they do not establish a legal minimum age for True Count. [Crapsee terms](https://v3.crapsee.com/legal/terms), [Blue Wind US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blackjack-trainer-all-in-one/id1470754366).

Store content ratings, contractual eligibility, children's privacy statements, and an app's actual age-check implementation answer different questions. The table keeps them separate. No apps were installed, accounts created, purchases made, or in-app age checks exercised during this research.

## Interpretation for True Count

The clearest counterexample to inferring eligibility from a store badge is Crapsee: its Play listing is Teen while its published terms require adulthood. Blue Wind shows the other ambiguity: its 13+ Apple rating coexists with privacy language aimed at excluding under-18s. Neither should be summarized as verified permission for teen accounts. [Crapsee Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.orangeblack.crapsee&hl=en_US&gl=US), [Crapsee terms](https://v3.crapsee.com/legal/terms), [Blue Wind listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blackjack-trainer-all-in-one/id1470754366), [Blue Wind privacy](https://bluewindservice.com/privacy-policy).

Apple currently places **infrequent simulated gambling at 13+** and **frequent simulated gambling at 18+** in its global rating definitions. Consequently, an app can receive an adult content rating without real-money wagering. True Count's actual training, simulations, and community content must be assessed on their own before submission; a competitor's rating does not determine its rating. [Apple rating definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/).

The user's MVP direction is **no product-wide 18+ requirement for the MVP, with launch eligibility revisited before first launch**. This research provides inputs for that later decision; it does not turn the deferral into an all-ages launch policy or select a replacement minimum age. Before launch, settle the intended audience, account eligibility, store classifications, any required age assurance, treatment of younger users' data, and whether community and purchasing features need different handling.

## Evidence limits and follow-up

- This is a dated public-source snapshot, not an audit of implementation or enforcement. Terms and store metadata may change. Recheck near submission.
- Apple observations use `/us/` listings. Google Play ratings were confirmed from direct HTML requests with `hl=en_US&gl=US`, including the page's `contentRating` metadata. The browsing service could not consistently retrieve localized URLs; unlocalized pages were also read for descriptions and policy links. Do not substitute PEGI values for US ratings.
- QFIT's exact current US Apple listing, Aw Craps!'s Android availability and policy content, Blue Wind's contractual threshold, and the scope of BJA's terms for standalone mobile use remain open source gaps. “Not found” means this check did not establish the policy, not that no restriction exists.
- Blue Wind's linked policy includes unrelated gambling promotional links after its policy text. The age statement above is accurately attributed to the store-linked page, but the page's maintenance and suitability as evidence should be rechecked before relying on it for a launch decision.
- No conclusion is drawn about the legality of admitting minors, privacy compliance, or required age-check technology. Competitor choices are evidence about competitors, not a substitute for True Count's prelaunch review.
